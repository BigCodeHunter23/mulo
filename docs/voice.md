# MULO's voice

MULO talks like someone who grew up on this music, not like a brand trying to
sound like them. The references are ones people already use. If a name needs
explaining, it's the wrong name.

## How it sounds

- **Culture, not slang.** Borrow from record shops, radio, mixtapes, the
  booth, the block. Don't write "fire", "lit" or "banger" into the interface.
- **Short.** A heading is two words where it can be.
- **Confident, not loud.** No exclamation marks, no emoji, no "epic".
- **Headings get the voice; controls stay plain.** Section names, features and
  badges carry the character. Buttons, navigation, form labels and error
  messages say exactly what they do: "Save review", "Log in", "My ratings".

## Named things

| Name | What it is |
|---|---|
| Your GOAT | Somebody's ranked top ten artists and albums |
| Heavy Rotation | What the most people on MULO are rating this week or month |
| Mixtape | Somebody's monthly recap: album and track of the month, on repeat, full tracklist |
| Undisputed | Highest rated on MULO |
| Staples | Most-listened albums, by ListenBrainz listens |
| Dig Deeper | Artists worth exploring |
| Word on the Street | The latest ratings from everyone |
| Top Shelf | Somebody's highest-scored albums, on their profile |
| Love / Nah | Reactions to somebody else's rating |

## Badges

Day Ones, Crate Digger, A&R, Liner Notes, Certified, The Crew, Time Traveller,
No Skips, Box Set. New badges follow the same rule: a name somebody into music
would recognise, earned by range or depth rather than volume alone.
