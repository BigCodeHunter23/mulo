# MULO's voice

MULO talks like someone who grew up on this music, not like a brand trying to
sound like them. The references are ones people already use. If a name needs
explaining, or reads like it's trying, it's the wrong name.

## Where the voice goes

- **Features and badges carry it.** The things people talk about by name:
  their GOAT, Heavy Rotation, their Mixtape, the badges.
- **Everything else stays plain.** Section headings, buttons, navigation, form
  labels and error messages say exactly what they are: "Top rated on MULO",
  "Just rated", "Save review", "My ratings". A renaming pass on the Discover
  headings (Undisputed, Staples, Dig Deeper, Word on the Street) was undone
  for being too forced.

## How it sounds

- **Culture, not slang.** Borrow from record shops, radio, mixtapes and the
  booth. Don't write "fire", "lit" or "banger" into the interface.
- **Short.** Two words where two will do.
- **Confident, not loud.** No exclamation marks, no emoji, no "epic".

## Named things

| Name | What it is |
|---|---|
| Your GOAT | Somebody's ranked top ten artists and albums |
| Heavy Rotation | What the most people on MULO are rating this week or month |
| Mixtape | Somebody's monthly recap: album and track of the month, on repeat, full tracklist |
| Top Shelf | Somebody's highest-scored albums, on their profile |
| Love / Nah | Reactions to somebody else's rating |
| Your Sound | The genres somebody rates highest, on their profile |
| Daily Versus | Two artists from the same era and lane, one pick a day. "Who you got?" |
| Raised On | The record somebody grew up on, picked by decade and scene; it becomes their picture |
| Rank-off | Head-to-head rounds that settle a tie for album or track of the month |

## Badges

Day Ones, Crate Digger, A&R, Liner Notes, Certified, The Crew, Time Traveller,
No Skips, Box Set. New badges follow the same rule: a name somebody into music
would recognise, earned by range or depth rather than volume alone.
